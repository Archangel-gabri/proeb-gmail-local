PROEB Gmail Local

A private, owner-operated desktop integration for finding and organizing the owner's Gmail messages. The integration runs locally on the owner's computer using the open-source gogcli client and Google's Gmail API.

What it does

  • Search and read messages and threads when requested by the owner.
  • Read and manage labels and mail-sorting filters.
  • Provide a local MCP interface for the owner's chosen assistant. Relevant mail content may be supplied to that assistant when the owner requests AI-assisted work.

Google OAuth is used instead of a Google password. Credentials are stored in the owner's system wallet. This website does not connect to Gmail, receive OAuth callbacks, or store email.

This is not a public hosted mailbox service. No ChatGPT subscription is required by the local integration. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or OpenAI.
